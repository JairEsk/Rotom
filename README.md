# Rotom

Rotom is a smart email management tool designed to keep your inbox clean and reclaim your storage space. It helps you easily identify and delete large, unnecessary emails that clutter your mailbox, with future plans to handle subscriptions, newsletters, tags, and automated retention policies.

## Features

-   **Find Large Emails:** Quickly scan your inbox to identify emails with large attachments that are taking up the most space.
-   **Storage Optimization:** Free up valuable email storage by deleting heavy, unwanted emails.
-   **Future Scope:** 
    -   Automated retention policies (e.g., delete emails older than a specific timeframe).
    -   Spam and newsletter subscription management.
    -   Smart tagging and categorization.
    -   Browser extension integration.
